## What is a new-school tattoo?

New-school is a tattoo style from the late twentieth century that treats every subject like an animated character: bulging shapes, exaggerated perspective, outlines that swell and shrink, and colors you'd find in a candy aisle. Where traditional is flat and formal, new-school is bouncy and loud, with highlights and glow effects doing a lot of the talking.

It's the style that lets a lava lamp have a rainbow chrome base and a cicada's wings go iridescent pink and teal. Nothing has to behave.

## Why is new-school a good fit for Chris?

Because his favorite thing to draw is a fun, surreal image with an animated, whimsical feel, and new-school is that idea as a style. The synthwave skull glowing inside its triangle, the smiley faces melting in green lava, the devil baby on the fiddle: these are pieces where the character has a personality before you've finished looking.

He also likes to cross the streams. The glowing acorn on its disc of golden lights is half new-school, half illustrative; the Fraggle in a gilded frame is a cartoon character treated like an oil portrait. If your idea is a mash-up, this is the right page.

## What ideas work well in new-school?

Characters, first and foremost: cartoons from your childhood, mascots, monsters, your pet as a superhero. Then objects with attitude, like a lava lamp, a Christmas tree with too many lights, or a skull that's clearly listening to synthwave. Food, drinks and candy work too. Sentiment isn't off-limits either; it just arrives wearing a grin.

The You Are My Sunshine piece on a calf is a good example: Snoopy and Woodstock on the doghouse, fireworks overhead, Charlie Brown below. Sentimental subject, zero sap.

## How big do new-school tattoos need to be?

New-school scales down better than most color styles because the outlines are thick and the shapes are simple, but the glow, chrome and gradients that make it sing need real estate. A single character fits a forearm or calf comfortably. Fill a whole upper arm and you get backgrounds, secondary characters and light effects.

Bright color also asks a little more of you during healing: sun and soaking are the enemies of saturation for the first few weeks. The [aftercare page](/aftercare/) covers it, and Chris goes over it again before you leave.
