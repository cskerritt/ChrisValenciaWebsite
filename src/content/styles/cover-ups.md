## Can my old tattoo be covered up?

Probably, with conditions. Cover-ups work best when the old tattoo is faded, small, or lightly saturated, and when you're open to a new piece that's noticeably larger and darker than what's there. Solid black, fresh heavy color, and large dense work are the hard cases. The only way to know is an in-person look.

Some tattoos are better candidates for a few sessions of laser fading before a cover-up; that opens up lighter designs and smaller sizes. You'll be told if that's the smarter route, even though it means waiting.

## How does a cover-up actually work?

Ink doesn't paint over ink; it layers. The new tattoo has to be built so the old lines fall inside its darkest shapes and busiest textures, which is why cover-ups lean on flowers, animals, smoke, water and shadow. The design gets drawn over a tracing of the old tattoo, so nothing is left to chance.

Expect the new piece to be darker in places than you'd choose for fresh skin. That's not a compromise in the drawing; it's the mechanism. A good cover-up hides the old tattoo and looks like it was always the plan.

## What can't a cover-up do?

It can't make skin blank again, and it can't turn a heavy black tribal band into a pastel watercolor. It can't guarantee zero show-through in every light for the rest of your life, either. What a good one does is hide the old tattoo from anyone who doesn't already know it's there.

If someone promises you a small, light, delicate cover-up over a big dark tattoo without seeing it, be suspicious. You'll get a straight answer here, even when the answer is "not yet, get it faded first."

## Which styles work best for covering a tattoo?

Neo-traditional and illustrative are the workhorses because both can carry dense shading and saturated color without looking heavy. Chris's habit of combining elements helps here: a snake winding through anemones or a tiger head in blossoms gives the design lots of dark, busy places to tuck an old tattoo. New-school can work when the old piece is light.

Black-and-grey cover-ups are possible too, though they usually need to go larger, since shadow is doing all the work.
