## How far is Chris Valencia from Pawtucket?

About twenty minutes, most of it on I-95. Get on southbound near downtown Pawtucket, ride through Providence, and take Route 10 where it splits off; Route 10 ends on Reservoir Avenue and the shop is a short way along Route 2. It's the longest drive of the cities listed on this site, and shorter than most lunch breaks.

Timing matters more than distance here. The I-95 run through downtown Providence is fine at mid-morning and miserable at five o'clock, so if you're booking a weekday session, a start time outside rush hour keeps the twenty minutes honest. Saturday mornings are usually smooth.

## Is a twenty-minute drive worth it for a tattoo artist?

For a specific artist, yes, and that's the only reason to make it. Pawtucket has shops. What it doesn't have is Chris's particular way of drawing: fun, surreal images with an animated, whimsical feel, in illustrative, neo-traditional and new-school styles. You'll wear the drawing for decades. The drive is twenty minutes.

Pawtucket knows a thing or two about craft. Slater Mill on the Blackstone is where American industry got going, the Modern Diner has been feeding people out of a streamlined railcar for generations, and the Hope Artiste Village mill is full of people making things by hand. Chris fits that lineage: years of tattooing in West Texas and around Atlanta before Rhode Island, and a portfolio where every piece was drawn for one person.

## What kind of work does Chris do?

Custom pieces in illustrative, neo-traditional and new-school styles, color or black-and-grey, usually with a twist: a trout bouquet rising out of a ring box, a cicada with iridescent wings, an origami penguin. Birds, animals, botanicals, pets and cartoon characters are regulars. He works all styles on request; those three are his home turf.

## How do I book from Pawtucket?

Use the booking form: idea, placement, rough size, budget range, timing, reference images. Powerline Tattoo is appointment-only, so there's no point driving down on spec; you'll hear back by email, agree on the design, and a deposit holds the date, with the amount confirmed when you book. Parking is street and lot, so ask what's easiest when you confirm.

Because it's a real drive, make the session count: bring references, wear something that clears the placement, and eat before you get on the highway. [Request a booking](/book/) or read [how pricing works](/pricing/).
