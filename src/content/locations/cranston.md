## Where in Cranston is Chris Valencia?

At Powerline Tattoo, 706 Reservoir Avenue, in the eastern half of the city. Reservoir Avenue is Route 2, the same road that runs past Garden City further south and up to the Providence line the other way. Route 10 comes down from the highway and ends on Reservoir Avenue, so it's easy to find.

Rolfe Square, the old Auburn shopping square, is a couple of minutes away. Roger Williams Park is just across the Providence line. Park Avenue crosses Reservoir Avenue nearby, so if you've ever cut across the city that way, you've been close to the chair without knowing it.

## What is it like to get tattooed at Powerline?

By appointment only, which means no waiting-room lottery and nobody hovering. You'll have a time, a stencil ready to check, and Chris's attention for the whole session. Parking is street and lot; confirm the current situation when you book. Bring a government ID, something to eat beforehand and a charged phone for the long stretches.

Chris is one of the resident artists at Powerline, and his chair is where the illustrative and cartoon-leaning work happens. The shop runs Monday through Saturday by appointment. If you're the walk-in type, the honest answer is to send a request and ask about same-week openings; there's no line to stand in.

## Why book a Cranston artist instead of driving into Providence?

Because the artist matters more than the zip code, and this one happens to be down the street. Chris draws fun, surreal images with an animated, whimsical feel, and he works custom, from your idea, in illustrative, neo-traditional and new-school styles. Providence has plenty of good shops. It doesn't have this particular chair.

He came to Rhode Island after years tattooing in West Texas and around Atlanta, so the portfolio has range: black-and-grey wildlife, watercolor birds, a ship sailing out of a teacup, a synthwave skull. Cranston already gave the world Del's frozen lemonade; a resident artist with that kind of sketchbook is a fair second export.

A few local notes, for the record:

- Knightsville and Garden City are a straight run down Route 2 or across Park Avenue.
- Edgewood and Pawtuxet Village: cut across on Park Avenue and you're there.
- Western Cranston: Route 37 east to Route 2, then north.

Send a booking request through this site with your idea, placement, rough size and a few reference photos, or call Powerline at (401) 369-7771.
