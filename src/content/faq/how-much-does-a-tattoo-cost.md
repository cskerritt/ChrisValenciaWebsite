Two things move the number most: how big the piece is and how much fine detail it carries. A palm-sized single subject and a full upper arm with a background are different days of work, even in the same style. The [pricing page](/pricing/) walks through what goes into a quote. No dollar figures appear on this site on purpose; a quote is only honest once the design exists.
