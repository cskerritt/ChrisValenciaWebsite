question: "Does Chris tattoo over scars?"
answer: "Sometimes. Scar tissue has to be fully healed and settled, it takes ink differently from normal skin, and raised or keloid scars are usually a no. It's assessed in person, like a cover-up. Mention the scar in your booking request with a photo, and the reply will tell you whether it's worth a consultation."

Designs over scars tend to work best when the scar becomes part of the drawing rather than something to hide, and when the style can handle a little unevenness in how the ink settles. Illustrative and neo-traditional both cope well with that.
